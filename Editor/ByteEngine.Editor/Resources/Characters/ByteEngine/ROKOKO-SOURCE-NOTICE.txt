Rokoko's resource page permits its free captures in personal and commercial
animation, VFX, game, 3D art and other 3D projects. These fifteen captures are
embedded as retargeted animations of ByteEngine's original mannequin models.
The source page advertises eleven clips; its public archive contains fifteen.

Changes: retargeted to the mannequin deform skeletons; scaled limb motion;
solved fixed-length arm chains; compressed thumb chains; baked at 30 FPS;
extracted fixed-root arm motion for the first-person mannequin.
No source character mesh, Rokoko software or raw FBX archive is included.
