ByteEngine TopDownController3D and TopDownCamera3D adapt ideas and algorithms from:
https://github.com/EugeneVorobyev/godot-duckov-controller
Source revision: 4b4f7e4dab1e7a8793db56e03a73a881966b696c
Files: Scripts/Player/player.gd, Scripts/Input/input_manager.gd,
Scripts/Camera/camera_rig.gd, Scripts/Camera/camera_utils.gd
Adaptation: native ByteEngine character motor, camera-relative movement, quaternion
facing, bounded camera-space look-ahead, frame-independent smoothing and zoom.
