Sound objects hold the Audio Source clip, volume, pitch, spatial setting, distance attenuation and mixer bus. Event sheets hold every trigger. Weapon/ability/health logic remains independent from audio. Removing an audio sheet silences its cues without disabling gameplay.

Covered cues: individual pistol/rifle/shotgun reports, equip, reload start/finish/cancel, empty trigger, confirmed hit, positional impact, successful pickup/drop, walking/running/jump/land, enemy steps/hit/death, melee swing, gunner bolt, player damage, Overdrive start/hum/stop, insufficient energy, full recharge, victory/defeat and quiet environmental machinery.
Enemy attack launch cues are inserted into their existing attack sheets at the corresponding swing or projectile action.

Native action: Audio / Playback > Play one-shot from sound object.
Choose the sound object and emission-position object. Pitch variation and maximum overlapping voices are editable per action. One-shots copy the template settings, overlap instead of restarting a shared source, and clean themselves up after playback. There is a global cap of 64 transient sound objects. Looping ambience and Overdrive hum use ordinary Play/Stop actions on their sound objects.

Sources: Kenney Sci-fi Sounds and Impact Sounds, CC0. Original licenses are included beside the converted assets. Selected effects were converted to mono PCM16 WAV and peak-normalized for native positional playback.
