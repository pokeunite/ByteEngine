Economy assumptions

Can the player afford their next answer?

Calculated route: combat-and-industry focus, saving for the next planned purchase instead of buying every affordable optional node. Purchases wait for their intended round, prerequisites and available cash. This is a planning model, not measured playtest evidence. Change the assumptions in the map sidebar to stress-test it.

Current game baseline: 18 ore + 7 per drill level, 6 enemies + 2 per completed round (capped at 24), 5 cr base refined price, 20 starting credits. The proposal adds later extraction milestones and more pressure instead of relying on damage inflation. These are proposed values, not installed game changes. Station upgrades are not duplicated. The delivery fallback prevents a poor exchange quote from blocking all progression. Deep-core permit proposes +70 ore under higher pressure.

Company research should unlock choices

Refinery and ammo-press stat upgrades stay at their machines. They do not appear again as company research. Player HP automatically restores at the end of a successful round; there is no medkit node and no Emergency Plating.

Scatter Specialist

Eight pellets may home gently toward up to three visible enemies in a 25-degree aiming cone, within 12 metres. Assign pellets across targets instead of dumping them into one. No through-wall seeking and no added total damage. Unassigned pellets remain normal ballistic shots. Smart Spread later adds a fourth target without creating extra pellets. These are proposed values to playtest.

Delivery or exchange

Delivery contracts lock a guaranteed price and complete in the background. The live exchange lets the player set target sales, manage reserved stock and look for a better net payout. Quantity and target are the only required choices. The chart shows the price needed to beat delivery after fees, a target line, recent trend and current buyer demand. Indicators describe conditions, not certain future profit.

Market research

Freight Licence increases guaranteed dispatch size from 40 to 80 ore. Demand Scanner reveals the next published demand cycle and countdown. Broker Agreement reduces live exchange fees from 3% to 1%. All three are now active in-game. Displayed live offers include fees; every completed sale records its gain or shortfall against the guaranteed buyer.

Economy assumptions

The round calculator uses the better of guaranteed delivery at 4.50 credits or the exchange quote after fees. Fees begin at 3%, falling to 1% with Broker Agreement. Expected defence replacement consumes refined ore only after a defence is unlocked. Refinery yield is held at 1.00 in this company-map model; improvements at the actual refinery are not duplicated here. All ore is assumed processed and sold: processing, freight delays, failed rounds and limited fill demand are not simulated, so this is an optimistic design estimate.

Pressure and the final target

Keep 30,000 credits before five active hours expire (ten displayed days). Money spent on upgrades slows that target but should earn useful options or improve later output. The proposed deep-core extraction increase is still required for later income; it is not an already-installed feature. Verify kill times, ammo supply, actual trading decisions and failure rates in playtests before applying these prices.
