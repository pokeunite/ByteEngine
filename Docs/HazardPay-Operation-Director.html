Operations, overclock and company deposits

Windows gameplay update · Plugin 3.68.0 · 3 November 2026

Waves

A 24-enemy operation authorizes 16 enemies first, then four more after three kills and the reinforcement gap. Enemies still enter at spaced intervals and the live cap is 16. Fast clearing shortens later spawn intervals; player or rig health below 30% extends the reinforcement gap. The selected enemy total and ore reward stay fixed.

The HUD separates active enemies from incoming enemies. After the final one or two hostiles remain for five seconds, markers help locate them, including off-screen positions. A one-second watchdog detects inactive, fallen and long-stalled living enemies. Where a safe authored front spawn with a route exists, recovery returns the same damaged enemy there rather than awarding a kill. Recovery counts and reasons are exposed on the operation component.

The original wave-31 incident has not been replayed from its exact runtime state. Tests cover the detected failure cases; if every spawn route is blocked, recovery reports that condition instead of spawning beside the player or silently completing the wave.

Optional overclock without a decision popup

A cleared normal operation secures its ore immediately. A 12-second contextual offer appears; ignore it, leave, or open another camp menu to continue normally. At the drill, tap Interact to open the next-operation menu, or deliberately hold it for 0.8 seconds to accept one bonus wave. A held key from the preceding wave cannot opt in automatically.

The bonus wave awards 50% extra ore against 50% as many enemies. It retains current player/rig health and ammunition. Clearing it restores normal post-operation health; failing it preserves the secured base raw haul and loses the unearned bonus. It cannot chain into another overclock. Offers are disabled during the onboarding tutorial and when cargo cannot hold the bonus. The operation component exposes these values.

Company hub → Account

The slider assigns 0–100% of ore-sale proceeds to a protected company account, in five-percent steps. Default: 50%. The remainder is spendable camp credits. Only protected deposits count toward the 30,000-credit target; purchases never spend that balance.

Each accepted shipment snapshots its allocation. Moving the slider affects future shipments. Integer rounding carries between sales, so splitting a sale into tiny shipments does not avoid deposits. Starting credits and research refunds are excluded. Existing saves retain their camp wallet, upgrades and timer; the new company account starts at zero unless already saved.

Verification

The director regression suite exercises reinforcement limits, pacing, recovery without free damage, base/bonus reward accounting, ignored offers, accidental holds, failed overclocks, legacy-save defaults, deposit rounding, locked shipment allocations, purchases, goal completion, slider mouse/keyboard input, tab hit areas and tap-to-continue input. Native screenshots were reviewed for the account, contract and overclock screens; the round-result overlap was removed.

Recommended next expansion

  1. R&D station first: an upgrade-map unlock that gates advanced defences and later industrial expansion.
  2. Additional machines afterward: limited construction slots and a shared power budget make production specialization a decision. Avoid unlimited duplication that invalidates machine upgrades.
  3. One pistol clone later: a visible, limited helper with upkeep and a clear role. It should help the operator, while aiming, prioritizing targets and protecting the rig remain the player's job.

These expansion ideas are recommendations, not implemented features. No new game export or browser-runtime rebuild was made for this update.
