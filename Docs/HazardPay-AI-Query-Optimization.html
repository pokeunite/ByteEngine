AI/query optimization, cheaper upgrades and spider cues

AI behaviour time fell from 13.3 to 3.5 ms/frame in the profiled 100-spider scene. Unprofiled cache-enabled runs reached 26.2–27.8 FPS. The 40 FPS target remains unmet; rendering is now the larger measured cost.

What changed in the engine

  • Physics query indexes are retained until collider membership, transforms, activation or primitive shape changes. Parent movement, resizing, reparenting and teleports remain immediately visible.
  • Query indexes are independent of simulation-step indexes, preserving stable query ordering and simulation contact behaviour.
  • Custom/imported geometry bounds are validated separately. Unchanged custom geometry no longer forces every primitive collider to be gathered for every ray or sweep.
  • Visual-only transform changes do not invalidate physics indexes. Collider-owning ancestor membership is refreshed when the hierarchy or components change.
  • AI target discovery uses a scene-local character registry and a nearest-candidate loop instead of scene-wide sorting. Disabled, inactive and removed characters are filtered correctly.
  • Explicit-target perception no longer allocates a one-element array. Hearing rejects out-of-range sounds before occlusion raycasts and iterates new noise events without allocating snapshots.
  • Reservation and behaviour cleanup reuse scratch lists instead of temporary LINQ arrays. Running attacks, movement speed, attack timing and perception distances are unchanged.
  • Public query snapshots still have independent lifetimes for nested callers. This pass does not claim zero-allocation queries or fully incremental per-leaf BVH updates.

Upgrade-map balance

All upgrade-map rank prices are 20% cheaper, rounded to the nearest credit (a positive price remains at least 1). This applies once through the same pricing function used by the purchase button, affordability check and credit charge. Existing ownership is preserved; no retroactive refunds or save resets were made. Machine-panel upgrades were not discounted.

Examples: 100 → 80 credits; 250 → 200; 1,000 → 800. Tune Upgrade Map Cost Multiplier on Mining Trade 3D; the default is 0.8.

Spider hit and death sounds

Real health damage now triggers a spatial spider-hit cue, and death triggers a separate spatial death cue. The previous generic spider impact subscription is skipped to avoid duplicated impacts. Cues use the existing configured body-impact and alien-growl assets as fallbacks, with independent optional sound references and volume controls on Mining Audio 3D. They share the bounded voice pool and have rate limits, so mass hits/deaths do not create unlimited voices.

Delivery contracts: current behaviour and recommendation

You were correct: the current board has three fixed definitions, and accepted delivery orders have no deadline. This is the current basic implementation, rather than a rotating timed contract board. The overall level target deadline is a separate system.

Recommended next change: refresh offers after operations and completed deliveries; keep three readable choices—small/reliable, medium/profitable and large/risky. Show quantity, locked payout and operations remaining. Accepted jobs should keep their price and requirements. Deadline expiry should release the order without deleting ore. The board, saves and tutorial must explain this consistently. These rotation/deadline changes were not included in this performance pass.
