# Freesound audition shortlist

39 candidates checked on 26 October 2026. Links open Freesound pages with their preview players. Proposed uses are based on uploader descriptions; final listening and selection remain with you. No audio was downloaded or imported.

38 entries are listed as CC0 on their Freesound pages. F3 is CC BY 4.0 and requires credit to Marcus Dellicompagni. Licence labels here record what the linked pages state; preserve source and licence records for any selected downloads.

Send the IDs you choose, e.g. W2, W5, C1, C2, M1, M3, U1. Alternatives do not all need to be used. Long source recordings will need trimming; looping and loudness still need testing in-game.
