Hazard Pay: contract expeditions

The production economy now runs against a committed wave schedule.

The loop

Choose a site → accept a six-wave contract → 45 seconds to prepare → fight → 45 seconds to refine, resupply and improve → repeat → safe camp after wave six.

  • Next waves start automatically. The timer continues in industry panels.
  • Start early at the drill for up to 15% extra ore; the bonus decreases with remaining preparation time.
  • No free ammunition or healing between waves. Health restores after the completed contract.
  • Pause still pauses the game. Rearrangement is allowed during preparation and closes when combat begins.
  • Cargo overflow loses excess wave ore. Expand, refine and sell to keep space available.

Industry and equipment

  • Refineries, ammo press and Field R&D can be used during combat; danger continues while panels are open.
  • Each refinery offers automatic loading at 35% longer processing time and unchanged yield. Manual loading remains faster. Each extra refinery retains its own load and upgrades.
  • The starting ammo press produces three rounds per six-second cycle with 35-round storage. Payload upgrades add two rounds per cycle.
  • Pistol, rifle and shotgun each gain a three-rank handling upgrade: magazine capacity, reload speed and accuracy. Research details show current and next values.
  • Drill output supports twelve upgrade levels, with explicit MAX feedback.

Defence roles

  • Sentry: 18 base damage every 0.55 seconds, 22m range. Mortar tuning stays unchanged.
  • Security drone: 12 base damage every 0.35 seconds, 16m range. It remains a finite-battery purchase.
  • Mines remain placed after discharge. Recharge depleted mines in Field R&D for ten refined ore each; no free wave reset. Enemy-destroyed mines still require replacement.
  • From wave three, some rig raiders become cyan-crested disruptors. Their fixed cyan warning zone gives one second to react before a three-second local defence shutdown. Kill them to prevent the pulse; committed mortar rounds still land.

Save behaviour

Existing company progress is retained. Preparation resumes with its saved countdown. Quitting during combat restarts that unpaid wave after five seconds with saved health and ammunition; individual enemy positions are not checkpointed.
