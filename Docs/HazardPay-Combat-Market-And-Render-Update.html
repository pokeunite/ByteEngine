1 November 2026 · Windows update · plugin 3.60.0

Clearer defences, stronger weapons and ore buying

Combat changes

  • Shotgun: 8 pellets × 9 base damage, up from 8 × 4. The tight 1.75° spread is retained. A confirmed nonlethal enemy hit starts a collision-swept 0.22-second burst at speed 12 and a matching short stagger. Unobstructed displacement is roughly 2.6 metres; walls and other collision can shorten it. Repeated pellets refresh a bounded burst instead of adding unlimited velocity.
  • AR: damage rises from 15 to 23. Pitch recoil falls from 0.6° to 0.18°, yaw from 0.25° to 0.06°, maximum recoil from 5° to 1.6°, with faster recovery.
  • Rig raiders: base speed reduced from 4.8 to 3.6; player hunters remain at 6. Wave modifiers still apply.
  • Gunshot mix: original clips retained. Pistol source volume 0.48 → 0.85, AR 0.32 → 0.68, shotgun 0.48 → 0.95. Reload clips and their mix are retained.

Defence feedback

  • Replacement cards explicitly say REFINED ORE.
  • Enemy destruction shows a five-second notification with the defence name and replacement cost, plus an alert sound and the existing destruction explosion.
  • Sentry, rotary gun and mortar have small world-positioned health bars. Low health changes the bar colour. Health bars are inspector-toggleable.
  • The destruction warning occupies a separate left-side area to avoid the operation, tutorial and arrange-camp header.

Economy and UI

  • Scatter Specialist: final displayed/purchase price is 180 credits. Its existing prerequisites and round requirement remain. The catalog base is 225 because the map-wide 0.8 multiplier applies once.
  • Ammo press and refinery: machine-panel upgrade prices are reduced by 20%, with inspector multipliers for later tuning. Existing upgrade levels and ownership are preserved.
  • Live market: use the MODE button to switch between selling and buying. Both raw and refined ore can be purchased. The quantity slider respects credits, shared cargo space and reserved space.
  • The buy price includes a visible 5% buying fee. The screen shows the current net sell price and the threshold needed for a profitable resale. The chart follows the selected buy/sell mode.
  • Purchases charge credits and add cargo immediately in one validated transaction. Invalid, unaffordable or oversized purchases make no partial changes. Buying is disabled during shipping and outside preparation.
  • Event-sheet actions expose buying ore; market-value readouts include buy price and maximum purchase quantity.
  • Delivery contract rotation/deadlines are unchanged in this update.

Manual browser-runtime refresh

Save and close the editor, open PowerShell in C:\Users\codex\ByteEngine, then run:

powershell -ep bypass -file .\Refresh-ByteEngine-BrowserRuntime.ps1

The wrapper forwards to the existing rebuild script with -BrowserOnly. It refreshes the runtime, not an exported game. Creating/testing this wrapper did not rebuild the browser runtime. Windows remains the normal development target.

Render/shadow submission optimization

Repeated simple animated actors can reuse material-compatible combined colour geometry and a combined opaque shadow mesh per shared pose. Shadow draws no longer need separate submissions solely because opaque parts use different colours. Skinning, sockets, triangle geometry and full shadow silhouettes remain intact.

Cutout/transparency, per-slot material overrides, hidden parts, advanced poses, invalid/reflected placements and alternate frame sinks retain safe fallbacks. The public submission API keeps its original signature for compiled plugin compatibility.
