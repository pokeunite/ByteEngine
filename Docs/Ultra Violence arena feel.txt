ULTRA VIOLENCE — MOVEMENT AND WEAPON FEEL PASS

Installed into Documents/ultra violence, Combat Prototype scene. No game export performed.

CONTROLS
WASD move at 7 m/s; Shift sprint at 10.5 m/s; Space jump.
Hold right mouse for Overdrive; E pickup; G drop; 1/2/3 equip; R reload.

MOVEMENT
An optional Arena Movement mode extends the existing native capsule motor.
Ground acceleration 95, friction 8, stopping speed 4, air acceleration 22, maximum air speed 13.
Jump speed 7.8, gravity 24, coyote time .12, jump buffer .13. Vertical camera FOV 72 degrees.
Ground releases brake promptly. Air releases preserve horizontal momentum; steering is directional and bounded.
No auto bunny hop, slide, wall run or dash is implied by this pass.

Research reference: OpenKCC, Nicholas Maltbie (MIT):
https://github.com/nicholas-maltbie/OpenKCC
https://github.com/nicholas-maltbie/OpenKCC/blob/main/Documentation/manual/kcc-design/kcc-movement.md
Reviewed its SimplifiedKCCWithJump sample, normalized camera-relative movement, capsule sweep/slide and grounding design.
Unity-dependent source is not installed into ByteEngine. The new directional acceleration/friction implementation is native to ByteEngine.

EDITING
FPS Character > Character Movement: Arena Movement and jump settings.
Assets/Events/Arena movement.byteevents: startup tuning action.
Assets/Events/Weapon inventory.byteevents: editable base/sprint speed actions.
FPS Camera > Field of View: camera setting.
Each gun > Weapon Definition: Projectile mode/speed/lifetime/range, trail width/length, muzzle bone/offset, flash asset/size, light intensity/range.
Weapons plugin actions: Set projectile speed and trail; Set muzzle light pulse.
Assets/VFX: individual Pistol, Rifle and Shotgun layered muzzle assets. Edit with the native VFX editor.

PHYSICAL SHOTS
All three weapons now use sphere-swept projectiles, camera aiming with muzzle-origin travel, and finite range/lifetime.
Short depth-tested streaks follow real projectile positions. Debug camera rays are disabled.
Projectile impacts now feed the existing Weapon shot hit / Last Ray Hit event-sheet workflow and damage once.
The fallback projectile visibility flag was fixed in the native launcher.
Barrel origins were measured on the imported original poses, then attached to weapon bones so animation moves the effects.
Flashes combine a hot core, short directional flame, sparse sparks, smoke and a brief shadow-free local light.

AFTERIMAGE PLUGIN
Reusable Afterimage v1.0.0. Added to the six animated enemy visual objects.
Assets/Events/Afterimages.byteevents checks the player's Overdrive variable and explicitly controls emission.
Use Enable afterimage emission / Configure afterimage trail / Clear afterimages in the event sheet.
Inspector exposes tint, opacity, interval, minimum distance, lifetime and ghost limit.
Installed settings: .075s interval, .38s fade, .045m minimum travel, four ghosts per source.
Snapshots freeze the actual deformed mesh pose. They do not duplicate controllers, AI, colliders or animations.
Real-time emission/fade stays smooth while enemy simulation runs at .25 speed.
Ghosts respect depth, cast no shadows and release their mesh resources after fading or scene unload.
This first-person setup shows trails on moving enemies. There is no third-person player body in the current rig.

RECORDED WEAPON AUDIO
The three active gunshot sound sources use genuine recordings from the CC0 Free Firearm Sound Library.
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
Creators: Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, Matthew Nanney.
Near-distance 1911 A_42P, AK-47 C_28P, Mossberg N_30P.
Trimmed attacks, mono conversion, 48kHz PCM16, level matching and tail fades.
Assets/Audio/SFX/Firearms contains active clips and source/license text.
Sound library > SFX Pistol / Rifle / Shotgun controls clips and mix.
Audio sheets are attached by weapon identity rather than slot number; user weapon ordering is retained.
The previous synthetic gunshot files remain unused for easy comparison/recovery.
